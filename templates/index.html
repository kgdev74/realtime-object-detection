💡 Quick Guide

  • Hold everyday objects (cell phone, bottle, cup, laptop) in front of the camera.
  • YOLOv8 automatically draws colored bounding boxes, labels, and confidence %.
  • If the camera doesn't start, verify camera permissions in Windows settings.
  • Press Ctrl + C in the terminal to cleanly terminate the server and release the camera.
